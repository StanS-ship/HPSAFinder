J-1 Visa Waiver Eligibility Check

Check whether a practice location meets the federal HPSA/MUA baseline that most state Conrad 30 J-1 visa waiver programs require.

Check a practice location

Enter a practice address to check whether it meets the federal shortage-area baseline (HPSA or MUA/MUP) that most state Conrad 30 J-1 visa waiver programs require as a starting point.

This checks the federal baseline only. It does not evaluate any individual state's additional Conrad 30 rules, slot availability, or specialty-specific requirements — see the note on the results page.
