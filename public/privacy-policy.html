2. Information We Collect

The categories below describe the personal information that we may collect. The categories actually collected depend on how you use the Site.

A. Search and calculator information

When you use the HPSA search or Medicare bonus calculator, you may enter:

  • A practice address, city, or ZIP code to check HPSA designation status
  • Provider type or specialty (e.g., physician, psychiatrist, dentist)
  • Estimated annual Medicare-paid amounts for bonus estimation

Do not enter patient names, Medicare beneficiary identifiers, medical-record numbers, diagnoses, claims containing patient identifiers, or other patient-level information. Search and calculator fields are not designed to receive protected health information.

B. Contact and referral information

If you contact us or voluntarily request an introduction to a hospital, medical practice, health system, staffing company, recruiter, placement agency, or other organization (a "Referral Partner"), we may collect your name, email address, and telephone number. Submitting a referral request is optional and is not required to use the free HPSA search or calculator.

C. Device and usage information

When you use the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect an Internet Protocol address, browser type and version, device type and identifiers, pages viewed, links clicked, search performance, referring page, date and time of activity, general location inferred from an IP address, and diagnostic, security, error, and performance information.

If you enter an address or other specific location, we may use it to identify the applicable geographic area, HPSA designation, map result, or calculation. Depending on the Site's configuration, the address or derived coordinates may be transmitted to our mapping or geocoding service provider solely to provide that function. We do not use entered addresses to infer a user's home location or for cross-context behavioral advertising.

D. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site may use cookies, local storage, pixels, software development kits, and comparable technologies to operate the Site, remember preferences, measure traffic, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and understand how users interact with the Site.

E. Information from others

We may receive information from service providers that host, secure, analyze, or support the Site; Referral Partners concerning the status of a referral; public and government sources; and a person or organization that submits information with authority to act for you. If you provide information about another person, you represent that you have authority to do so and have supplied any required notice and obtained any required consent.

3. Information We Do Not Seek

The Site does not require user accounts and is not designed to collect:
